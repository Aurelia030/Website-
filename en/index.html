What happens after my enquiry?

Aurelia reviews your details and gets back to you with an initial assessment of the property, rental price and possible next steps.

Is there a charge for the initial enquiry?

No. The initial enquiry is non-binding and helps us assess your property.

Do I need professional photos already?

No. Simple phone photos are enough for a first impression. If appropriate, Aurelia can arrange photos and a 360° tour.

What exactly does Aurelia take care of?

Depending on the property, Aurelia can help with assessments, photography, 360° tours, furnishing, cleaning, marketing, applicant shortlisting and ongoing management.

Do you have fixed prices?

Services depend on the property and the scope of work. After your enquiry, Aurelia will discuss which arrangement suits your needs.

Does Aurelia operate only in Berlin?

Our focus is Berlin. Depending on the property, we may also be able to help elsewhere in Germany.
