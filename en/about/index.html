Matteo HauschildFounder and Managing Director

Move in, unpack and feel at home – that is how a stay in a furnished apartment should begin. With Aurelia, I want to offer a furnished home where the details are taken care of and there is a personal point of contact whenever questions arise.

My background is in hospitality. Before founding Aurelia, I had already helped several property owners refurbish and let their properties. That experience made me want to combine the design of living spaces with personal support for owners and tenants as a profession.

I am also studying Hotel and Event Management. I combine what I learn about organisation, service and guest experiences with my practical experience. My aim at Aurelia: well-managed properties, clear processes and a personal point of contact.
