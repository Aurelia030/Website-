Data controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data is Aurelia Apartments UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Wiclefstraße 66, 10551 Berlin, Germany, represented by its managing director Matteo Hauschild. Email: info@aurelia-apartments.de; telephone: +49 157 72119082.

Hosting and email

Our public website and email services are hosted by STRATO. When you access the website, technically necessary data is processed, in particular your IP address, the time of access, the resources requested and browser information. This serves to deliver the website and ensure its stability and security, on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Before publication, the precise STRATO contracting entity, the actual log data, its retention period and the data processing agreement must be checked against our hosting contract and completed.

Contact and enquiries

If you contact us by email, telephone or the Tally form, we process your contact details, the content of your enquiry and any files you provide to deal with your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry concerns a contract with you, and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our interest in responding to enquiries. The data is erased once it is no longer needed to handle your request, unless statutory retention obligations or legitimate grounds for continued storage apply. Specific deletion periods and procedures, including those for files stored with Tally, must be established before publication.

LinkedIn links

The LinkedIn buttons are ordinary links to external profiles and pages. No LinkedIn widgets or tracking scripts are loaded when you view our website. A connection to LinkedIn is established only when you open a link. LinkedIn’s privacy policy applies on its website.

Privacy choices and external services

Google Maps, Matterport and Tally are loaded only with your consent and after you select the relevant load button. We store your choice under the key aurelia-privacy-v1 in your browser’s local storage for 180 days so that we can respect your privacy choices. Optional content is embedded on the basis of Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where required, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG. You may withdraw your consent at any time with future effect using the privacy settings in the footer. Loaded external content is then closed and further loading is blocked. This does not erase data already transmitted or cookies stored by the provider. The website does not integrate its own analytics or marketing tools.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. Where we process data on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may object to processing for direct marketing at any time. Please use the contact details above to exercise these rights. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information; further information is available at datenschutz-berlin.de.

External content and browser storage — current draft

The apartment photos, portrait and AI-generated illustrations are embedded directly in the website. These images do not establish a connection to an external image provider. Fonts are served locally. Your choice of optional external services is stored locally in this browser for 180 days. You can change it at any time using the privacy settings in the footer. The website itself does not integrate analytics tools. Neighbourhood maps on the apartment pages are embedded only after you click “Load Google Maps”. This establishes a connection to Google. Google may process technical data such as your IP address and set cookies. The map search includes only a street name or district, not a building number. The exact apartment address is not transmitted to the map service. Further information: Google Privacy Policy. In the renovation section, two Matterport 360° tours are embedded only after you click “Load tour”. This establishes a connection to my.matterport.com. Matterport may process technical data such as your IP address and set cookies. Alternatively, you can open the tours on the provider’s website using the direct links. Further information: Matterport privacy policy. On the enquiry page, the form provided by Tally is embedded only after you click “Load form”. This establishes a connection to tally.so; technical data, particularly your IP address, may be transmitted to Tally and cookies may be set. When you submit the form, Tally processes your details and any uploaded files so that we can handle your enquiry. You can also use the direct link to open the form on Tally. Further information is available at Tally privacy policy. Full information on the controller, legal bases, retention, recipients and data subject rights must be added before publication. When contacting us by email or telephone, you use the respective application.
